Internet delivered through light, carried to the room by your mains wiring.

Power Line Communication moves data fast over the wires already in the walls. Visible Light Communication then hands it to a device through a lamp. We are building and testing the link between the two.

How it works

Data takes three steps from the router to your device. The mismatch between the first and last is the interesting part.

1. Over the power line

HomePlug adapters send data through the mains wiring. It is fast, but interference makes it arrive in bursts, with short dropouts.

2. Through a buffer

A small memory holds data while the power line is busy and releases it at the pace the light link can take.

3. Out through light

An LED flickers too fast to see and carries the data to a photodiode. Reliable, but much slower than the wire.

Size the buffer

The power line stalls now and then, then sends everything it was holding at once. Choose how much memory the buffer gets. Too little and data is lost in the burst. Memory costs money and chip space, so find the smallest size that keeps losses under 1%.
